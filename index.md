# About


I'm a permanent researcher (MCF) in mathematics at [Université de Paris](https://u-paris.fr/en/) (P7, LPSM), working on probabilistic fields, with a nonstandard view on the future of mathematics and scientific higher education. I was co-founder and AI lead of [Bang! vertical comics](https://bang-comics.com/en). 


In 2026, I am an invited researcher at CMAP in École Polytechnique. 

### Non-academic 

- I wrote an [essay](https://legrandcontinent.eu/fr/dimanches/lia-et-la-joie-obscure-des-mathematiques/) in French for Le Grand Continent, around the maths crisis. 

- I was invited by the talented Lloyd Chéry to talk about maths, AI and Science-fiction in his podcast [C'est plus que de la SF](https://www.cestplusquedelasf.com/podcasts/ia-mathematiques-science-fiction), still in French.
